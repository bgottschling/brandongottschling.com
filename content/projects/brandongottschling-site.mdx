> **Why**  
> One home for research notes, projects, and writing—lightweight, fast, and easy to grow. It’s a “living workshop” for shipping in public.

## Stack Overview

- **Stack:** Next.js 15 (App Router), MDX + filesystem content, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, `remark-gfm`, `rehype-slug`/`autolink-headings`, Vercel.  
- **Design:** warm, minimal UI; high-contrast cards; animated **BackgroundNetworkStable** canvas with “harmony” moments.

---

## What changed in v0.4

**CV: executive-ready and printable**
- New **CV header bar** with mobile-aware collapse/expand and sticky behavior (flush with site nav on scroll).
- Toggleable **Experience / Skills** views; **Executive Summary** at the top for fast scanning.
- Per-role **Scope** and **Key Results** blocks (metrics are draft→validated), rendered above highlights.
- **Download PDF** now uses a server route (`/api/pdf/render`) that boots headless Chrome, waits for fonts/canvas, honors `@page`, and captures the live background as a watermark. Includes a **“Preparing…”** loading state and robust errors.

**Scrim & readability**
- Column scrim refined: rectangular lane with **left/right edge fades**, slight tint, and balanced blur so text stays crisp.
- Z-stack formalized (canvas `-z-30` → scrim `z-0` → content `z-10+`) across pages for consistent layering.

**Filter shell polish**
- Sidebar sits further from the reading lane; content remains centered.  
- Mobile keeps the filter grid in a sheet/drawer; keyboard and screen reader flow verified.

**DX & safety rails**
- Fixed “invalid element type” issues by aligning **default vs named exports** for CV components.  
- Hardened PDF route with `preferCSSPageSize`, font readiness, and a simple “pdf-export” CSS hook.

## Highlights (implementation notes)

**New / updated components**
- `src/components/cv/CvHeaderBar.tsx` – sticky, mobile-savvy header with actions (PDF, Print, vCard, Copy email).  
- `src/components/cv/ExecutiveSummary.tsx` – compact summary card (Client).  
- `src/components/cv/ExperienceView.tsx` – renders **Scope / Key Results / Highlights** with badges.  
- `src/components/cv/SkillsView.tsx` – tidy skill buckets with search.  
- `src/app/api/pdf/render/route.ts` – **puppeteer-core + @sparticuz/chromium** in prod, plain `puppeteer` in dev; waits for fonts/canvas; prints A4 with background.

**Scrim**
- `src/components/EdgeScrim.tsx` – **column-blur** variant (linear mask only on sides, no circular vignette) with tint/saturate controls.

**Hooks**
- `src/hooks/useHeaderOffset.ts` – aligns sticky CV bar under site nav.  
- `src/hooks/useSmartCollapse.ts` – mobile collapse logic (tap to expand; auto-minimize on scroll).

## Before / After (UX at a glance)
