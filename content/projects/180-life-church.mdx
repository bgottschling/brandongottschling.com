> **Why**
> Every skill is a gift. When a local church needs a digital home that actually reflects who they are, that is an opportunity to serve with purpose. This project is about using the craft God gave me to help a growing community reach more people and make a real difference.

## The Church

180 Life Church is a warm, non-denominational community in Bloomfield, Connecticut. Their doors are open to everyone. The heart behind 180 Life is simple: know God, find real connection, and take the next step together. They are building something life-giving, and their digital presence should reflect that same energy.

## The Work

This is a ground-up digital renovation. The goal is a modern, accessible, and inviting web presence that feels like walking through their doors for the first time. Fast load times, clear calls to action, mobile-first design, and a visual identity that communicates warmth and welcome before a single word is read.

## Where We Are

We are in the discovery phase right now, learning the community's story, understanding what matters most to the congregation and leadership, and mapping out the experience we want to create together. Good foundations lead to good outcomes.

## Stack

- **Core:** Next.js, Tailwind, Vercel
- **Content:** Structured for easy updates by non-technical staff
- **Hosting:** Edge-deployed for fast load times everywhere
