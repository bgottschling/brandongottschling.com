> **Why**
> Every skill is a gift. When a local church needs a digital home that actually reflects who they are, that is a chance to serve with purpose. This project is me using the craft God gave me to help a growing community reach more people.

## The Church

180 Life Church is a warm, non-denominational community in Bloomfield, Connecticut. Their doors are open to everyone. The heart behind 180 Life is simple: know God, find real connection, and take the next step together. They are building something life-giving, and their website should feel like that.

## The Work

A ground-up rebuild. Modern, accessible, mobile-first, fast, and a visual identity that communicates welcome before a single word is read. Just as important: the people who run the church need to run the site without me on the phone.

## Where We Are

Discovery is done and the site is built. Since the spring it has picked up:

- Sermons synced from the church’s YouTube channel, with retries so a quota hiccup doesn’t break a build, plus an audio player for listening on the go.
- Giving, leadership, and support-team pages the staff update themselves through the CMS.
- A contact flow with verification and spam protection.
- A security pass before launch: response headers, XSS hardening in the CMS, and personal emails pulled out of the public site.
- Hosting cost tuning so pages revalidate on a sensible schedule instead of rebuilding constantly.

What’s left is hand-off. Documentation, a walkthrough with the team, and the boring-but-important work of making sure nothing depends on me.

## Stack

- **Core:** Next.js, Tailwind, Vercel
- **Content:** A CMS the staff edit directly; sermons pulled from the YouTube Data API
- **Hosting:** Edge-deployed with incremental revalidation
